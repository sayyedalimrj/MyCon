## Engineering comparison / outcome

| Claim / decision | Scientific authority | Code evidence / test protocol | Field interpretation / risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photo/SfM occupancy may detect installed elements with occlusion model | Golparvar-Fard et al. 2015 | optional hash-verified A Stage8 geometry manifest + element_visibility / camera refs; tests verify invalid geometry -> null observability | out_of_fov and occluded are not not_built; incorrect camera pose and weak scale invalidate an element |
| Geometry-only Scan-vs-BIM cannot find relocated MEP reliably | Bosché et al. 2015 | Open3D registration demonstration; no automatic assignment from AABB; require IFC mapping | missing/repositioned MEP must remain unknown until independent scan-to-BIM QA |
| Operation-level state differs from physical element completion | Han & Golparvar-Fard 2014/2015 | reviewed image_sha256 + sample IDs; optional operation stage/decision evidence | stage material cues are review aids; do not substitute visual coverage for quantity survey |
| Economic physical progress and schedule comparison | 4D BIM operation-level papers plus formal project measurement/BOQ policy | existing approved_quantity + activity_id/IFC GlobalId + WBS approved weights + approved dated baseline; scientific_progress.v2; dedicated round2 tests | signed numeric progress and partial-identification bounds; project KPI null if BOQ/baseline incomplete |
| Independent expert comparison must use held-out truth | experiment design, not directly inferred from source-paper published accuracy | site-disjoint held-out IFC GlobalIds and activity expert report; match as-of time, count missing predictions, separately score categorical F1 and numeric MAE | reporting N/denominators prevents cherry-picking and train/test leakage; publication gate blocked until real GT |

Alternatives: (A) scan-vs-BIM registered point/visibility statistics: useful for independent observation QA but occlusion/scale vulnerable and insufficient for quantities; (B) documented quantity-based progress weighted by approved BOQ/WBS and compared with approved 4D baseline: reliable auditable percent when quantity provenance exists but may lag observations; (C) operation-level material recognition: useful for formwork/rebar/concrete stages with calibrated supervised images, not a substitute for certified measurements. MyCON adopts (B) as estimate; (A)/(C) only evidence hints with fail-closed gates.

**Limitations:** three supplied DOI links and fourth were resolved against institutional/publisher metadata; no full-paper reimplementation or independent numerical benchmark claimed. Product of these sources does not certify the supplied MyCON room as metric, registered, IFC-mapped, built, unbuilt or publication-ready. Real field GT/BOQ/IFC/video not available to this B workstream.
