## Peer-reviewed primary literature — DOI checked
1. **Schnabel, R.; Wahl, R.; Klein, R. (2007)**, “Efficient RANSAC for Point-Cloud Shape Detection,” *Computer Graphics Forum* 26(2):214–226. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2007.01016.x . Robust plane/primitive proposals on unorganized noisy data, but a plane's infinite mathematical extent is NOT observed construction surface. **Adopt** local RANSAC+finite occupancy+unknown classification; test interruptions, slanted walls and multiple disconnected islands. Publisher: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2007.01016.x
2. **Yeshwanth, C.; Liu, Y.-C.; Nießner, M.; Dai, A. (2023)**, “ScanNet++: A High-Fidelity Dataset of 3D Indoor Scenes,” *ICCV* pp. 12–22. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV51070.2023.00008 . Real indoor geometry and DSLR/iPhone RGB(-D) registered to reference laser scans; ambiguity annotations demonstrate that semantic labels require evidence. **Adapt as independent future benchmark**, NOT a surrogate for MyCON construction-site survey or training/test leakage. Primary: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Yeshwanth_ScanNet_A_High-Fidelity_Dataset_of_3D_Indoor_Scenes_ICCV_2023_paper.html
3. **Huang, B.; Yu, Z.; Chen, A.; Geiger, A.; Gao, S. (2024)**, “2D Gaussian Splatting for Geometrically Accurate Radiance Fields,” *SIGGRAPH 2024 Conference Papers*, article 32. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3641519.3657428 . 2D oriented surfels, depth/normal regularization and meshing offer a meaningful alternative to naïve 3DGS-centre-as-surface. **Defer** new GPU training/TSDF until authorized, calibrated cameras and independent ground-truth support. Even a generated mesh may contain unsupported filled holes. Paper: https://www.cvlibs.net/publications/Huang2024SIGGRAPH.pdf
4. **Kazhdan, M.; Hoppe, H. (2013)**, “Screened Poisson Surface Reconstruction,” *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 32(3):29. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/2487228.2487237 . Oriented normals→watertight global mesh. **Reject as a sole evidence source** for an unfinished room because Poisson fills unknown/occluded wall and opening surfaces; retain as opt-in visualization subject to original point support. Author paper: https://www.hhoppe.com/screenedpoisson.pdf
5. **Stuart, L. A. G.; Morton, A.; Stavness, I.; Pound, M. P. (2025)**, “3DGS-to-PC: 3D Gaussian Splatting to Dense Point Clouds,” *ICCV Workshops*, pp. 3789–3798. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW69036.2025.00395 . Samples/render-weights Gaussians to obtain dense candidate points; **candidate research baseline only**, not raw Gaussian centres and not independently validated as-built truth. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/3D-VAST/html/Stuart_3DGS-to-PC_3D_Gaussian_Splatting_to_Dense_Point_Clouds_ICCVW_2025_paper.html

## Engineering comparison
| Method | Reconstruction evidence required | Best use for unfinished room | Failure/unknown policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local RANSAC + SVD + finite observed support | Dense/Depth-fused cloud, source frame, thresholds | Candidate wall/slanted panels; residual/normal audit | Preserve small, split and missing patches; candidate only, never complete infinite wall |
| Oriented PCA normals + region growth | sufficiently dense nonuniform points | Find orientation and small panels missed by dominant RANSAC | Unoriented normal sign does not determine floor/ceiling; edge discontinuity/noise can merge regions |
| Two-plane intersections + local near-line evidence | TWO observed near-line spans; image checks ideally | Partial observed wall-wall junction | No gap-bridging, no extrapolated corner promoted without 3D+image support |
| Screened Poisson | correct oriented normals/coverage | Optional shape visualization in well-observed regions | Watertight hallucinated surfaces and erased holes: never field-confirm construction |
| 2DGS / TSDF mesh | calibrated multi-view RGB/depth/poses and GPU | Future alternative surface hypothesis | TSDF fills missing observations and scene poses can drift; preserve independent source cloud, no new training without permission |
| ScanNet++ high-res indoor benchmark | independent surveyed GT, aligned RGB images | Future controlled semantic/geometry repeatability benchmark | Cross-building/capture split; real MyCON quality not inferred from another dataset |

## Claim → paper → code → experiment → civil interpretation
| Claim to audit | Scientific source | Code evidence | Required experiment | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finite planes are robust to noise | Schnabel 2007 | MyCON Stage7 + Open3D RANSAC | Same support under perturbation, density, occlusion, tilted walls | Fit stability diagnostic, not field accuracy |
| Mesh != observed site | Kazhdan 2013; Huang 2024 | Open3D meshing; 2DGS render | Compare mesh vertices/triangles to independently reviewed observed raw samples | Keep unobserved holes as unknown |
| Room semantic labels need reference views | ScanNet++ 2023 | MyCON camera/image evidence schema | Known 3D→2D projections with calibrated intrinsics, audited visibility and reviewed crops | Do not call horizontal plane floor/ceiling from unsigned normals |
| Gaussian centres != measured surface | Huang 2024; Stuart 2025 | 2DGS and 3DGS-to-PC algorithms | Matched source cameras, independently surveyed samples/held-out view | Prior 3DGS PLY cannot itself certify structural dimensions |
| Corner status must not be overstated | Schnabel 2007 | MyCON bounded edges + trihedral support | False corner tests with separate point islands and occlusion | Candidate/extrapolated/unknown pending human review |
